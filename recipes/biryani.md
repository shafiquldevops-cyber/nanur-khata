# Nanu's Eid Biryani

## Ingredients
- 1 kg basmati rice
- 1 kg chicken
- 3 onions
- 1 cup yogurt
- Ginger and garlic paste
- Biryani spices
- Salt and cooking oil
- 4 green cardamom pods

## Preparation
1. Wash and soak the rice.
2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
3. Fry the onions until golden brown.
4. Cook the chicken with the spices.
5. Layer the rice and chicken in a pot.
6. Cook until the rice and chicken are fully done.

## Family Story
Nanu makes this special biryani for Eid.
The whole family gathers around the table to enjoy it.
