# Chittagong Mezban

## Ingredients
- Beef
- Onion
- Garlic and ginger
- Red chilli powder
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Cooking oil

## Preparation
1. Cut the beef into pieces.
2. Mix the beef with spices, onion, garlic, and ginger.
3. Cook slowly until the beef becomes tender.
4. Adjust the seasoning and serve hot with rice.

## Family Story
Mezban is a traditional feast associated with the hospitality and food culture of Chittagong.
