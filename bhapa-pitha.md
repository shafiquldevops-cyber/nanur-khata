# Nanu's Bhapa Pitha

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1 cup grated coconut
- 2 cups date palm jaggery (gur)
- A pinch of salt
- Warm water as needed

## Preparation
1. Mix the rice flour with a little salt and water.
2. Prepare the grated coconut and jaggery filling.
3. Place the filling between layers of rice flour.
4. Steam the pitha until fully cooked.
5. Serve warm during winter.

## Family Story
Nanu prepares bhapa pitha on cold winter mornings.
The smell of date palm jaggery brings everyone together.
