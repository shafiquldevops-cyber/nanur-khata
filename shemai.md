# Nanu's Eid Shemai

## Ingredients
- 200 g roasted shemai
- 1 litre milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 cardamom pods
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- Raisins and nuts

## Preparation
1. Heat the ghee in a pan.
2. Lightly fry the shemai.
3. Boil the milk with cardamom.
4. Add sugar and shemai to the milk.
5. Cook until the shemai becomes soft.
6. Garnish with raisins and nuts.

## Family Story
Nanu prepares sweet shemai for Eid.
Everyone enjoys it after the family meal.
